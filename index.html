How to improve typing speed and accuracy

Build typing speed through regular, deliberate practice. Start at a comfortable pace, keep your eyes on the passage, and prioritize accurate keystrokes before trying to type faster. IQTyping provides timed practice, live words-per-minute (WPM), accuracy feedback, and session history stored in your browser.

1. Practice consistently

Complete one or two focused sessions each day. Short, repeatable practice is easier to sustain than occasional long sessions.

2. Accuracy before speed

Slow down when errors increase. Correct technique and fewer mistakes help build reliable speed over time.

3. Use timed tests

Try one-minute tests for quick checks and longer tests to practice maintaining a steady pace. Compare results across sessions rather than judging yourself by one run.

4. Practice useful material

Use word drills to build rhythm, motivational quotes to maintain focus, and GK/GS passages to type useful study content.

What do WPM and accuracy mean?

Words per minute (WPM) estimates typing speed using the common convention of five characters per word. Accuracy estimates the share of typed characters that match the displayed passage. Both measures matter: high speed with frequent errors may not be useful in exams or everyday work.
